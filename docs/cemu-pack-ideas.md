# Cemu graphic packs to port to SLOW

The ones chosen on 2026-10-31 from the Breath of the Wild packs in
[cemu_graphic_packs](https://github.com/cemu-project/cemu_graphic_packs) (`src/BreathOfTheWild`). Neither has been tried here.

Cemu's packs target the Wii U game, so they don't apply as they are: shader packs have to be re-done against the Switch's
shaders, as Clarity was. Check each pack's licence before porting anything from it.

| Pack | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Graphics: Shadow Draw Distance | How far the sharp and far shadow ranges reach |
| Enhancements: Reflections, Reflection Range | Reflection quality and how far they reach |
